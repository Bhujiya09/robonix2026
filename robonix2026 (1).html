About the Festival

Where Innovation
Meets Imagination

ROBONIX 2026 is Jusco School South Park's flagship annual Tech Fest — a grand celebration that brings together the brightest young minds to compete, create, and connect across a vast spectrum of disciplines.

From cutting-edge robotics and AI challenges to electrifying esports battles, expressive cultural performances, and mind-bending intellectual contests — ROBONIX is where future innovators are born.

Held across two action-packed days, this festival is open to school students from across the region, providing a platform to showcase talent, collaborate, and experience the thrill of competitive excellence.

Rules & Regulations

Guidelines & Policies

⚙ General Rules

  • Participants must carry valid school ID cards at all times.
  • Registration is mandatory for all events — no spot entries without prior registration.
  • Participants are expected to report to their event venue 30 mins before start time.
  • Judges' decisions are final and binding in all competitions.
  • Any form of malpractice or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Coordinators must be informed of any grievances within 10 minutes of result declaration.
  • Prizes will be awarded during the valedictory ceremony on Day 2.

🤖 Tech & Esports Rules

  • All hardware must comply with specified size and weight restrictions per event.
  • Teams are responsible for their own equipment and power sources.
  • Internet access will be restricted during coding events — use of AI tools is prohibited.
  • Esports participants must use tournament-designated systems only.
  • Game accounts must be at the required minimum level as specified per event.
  • Any hardware/software tampering will result in disqualification.
  • Robotics kits must be shown to organizers for pre-event verification.

🎭 Cultural & Creative Rules

  • Performances must not exceed the allotted time limits; a siren will signal at 1 minute left.
  • Props, music tracks, and costumes must be arranged by participants themselves.
  • Content must be suitable for a school audience — no profanity or objectionable material.
  • Artwork submitted must be original and created during the allotted time.
  • Photography participants must use school-approved camera equipment only.
  • Scripts for drama/debate must be submitted 1 hour before the event begins.

🏆 Sports Rules

  • Appropriate sports attire and footwear is mandatory for all sports events.
  • Age-group eligibility applies — participants must fall in the specified class range.
  • Warm-up sessions are the responsibility of participants and their coaches.
  • Results of indoor sports will follow standard national federation rules.
  • Track events will use standard lane assignments determined by draw of lots.
  • Any disputed calls must be raised with the sports referee immediately.

📋 Code of Conduct

  • Maintain decorum and respect towards fellow participants, judges, and staff.
  • Vandalism or damage to school property will be subject to strict disciplinary action.
  • Social media posts must use the official hashtag #ROBONIX2026.
  • Photography of results before official announcement is strictly prohibited.
  • Outstation participants must register with the hospitality desk upon arrival.
